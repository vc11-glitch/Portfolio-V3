Approach

I don't think in channels.
I think in flow.

Most of the time the issue isn't missing output — it's broken connection between pieces. Here's how I work through it.

🔍
Step 01
Audit
Identify where the flow breaks — content, email, or page. Find what's unclear before anything else.
✂️
Step 02
Simplify
Remove noise first. Reduce before adding. Every piece should earn its place in the story.
🔗
Step 03
Connect
Link content, email and pages into one coherent narrative. Make the hand-offs work.
⚡
Step 04
Activate
Less friction, more momentum. The goal isn't more activity — it's better results.
